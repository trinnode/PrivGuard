About PrivGuard

Understanding privacy, documenting incidents, finding support.

Our Mission

PrivGuard is a privacy incident reporting system built for Nigerian university students. We provide a structured, confidential platform to document digital privacy violations, classify harms using a research-backed taxonomy, and connect with relevant support resources.

Privacy violations in digital spaces are underreported, especially within academic communities. Students may not know their rights, may fear retaliation, or may lack access to appropriate reporting mechanisms. PrivGuard bridges this gap.

How It Works

1. Report, Use our guided multi-step form to document an incident. You can report anonymously or with your account.

2. Classify, Identify the psychological and tangible harms you experienced using a taxonomy adapted from academic privacy research.

3. Access Support, Browse curated resources for legal rights, mental health support, digital safety, and campus-specific guidance.

4. Export, Generate structured PDF reports with reference codes for your records, research, or advocacy.

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our foundation. PrivGuard is designed with the following principles:

Confidentiality, Incident data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your identity is never shared without explicit consent.

Anonymous reporting, You can submit an incident without creating an account. Anonymous reports are assigned a reference code for your records. You can also request identity concealment on any report from your settings.

Data minimisation, We collect only the information needed to document and classify privacy harms. You control what you share.

Compliance, PrivGuard operates in alignment with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and respects the constitutional right to privacy under Section 37 of the 1999 Constitution.

Contact

PrivGuard is developed as a research and advocacy tool for digital privacy rights. For questions, feedback, or support, please reach out through the available resources or contact your campus privacy advocacy group.
